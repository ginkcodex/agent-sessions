# LL-01 — Content-based pane sizing

## Scope

Pure, unit-tested layout math in `frontend/src/lib/layoutSizes.ts`. The
sidebar and session list keep their proven defaults and floors; content
measurement covers the transcript header, the pane that runs out of room:

- `transcriptNeed(rows, measure)`: the width the transcript header needs to
  show its rows unwrapped — shrinkable items (the cwd path) count only their
  minimum width, the rest their measured text at the header's computed fonts —
  plus chrome, clamped between a hard floor (380 px) and a cap (800 px) so one
  long path cannot take over the window. Measurement goes through an injected
  `MeasureText`; `createMeasure()` provides the canvas implementation and
  measures nothing without a DOM.
- `fitToWidth(windowWidth, widths, floors)`: keeps the transcript at
  `max(380 px, transcriptNeed)` when the window narrows — the sidebar and
  session list give up width, the pane further above its floor first. A window
  too small for all three floors lets the transcript take the hit.

## Acceptance criteria

- Needs are capped and floors are enforced: no input (very long paths, huge
  fonts, tiny windows) yields a pane below its floor or above its cap.
- `fitToWidth` leaves panes untouched when they fit, shrinks the most
  over-provisioned pane first, and never goes below a floor.
- No DOM is required for the math; measurement degrades to the minimums.

## Tests

- `frontend/test/layout-sizes.test.mjs`: need math (gaps, padding, widest row,
  flex minimums, floor/cap), fit precedence, shrink order, exact-fit boundary,
