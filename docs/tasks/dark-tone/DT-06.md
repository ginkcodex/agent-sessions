# DT-06 — Lighter dark surfaces

- **Status:** ✅ Done
- **Estimate:** 0.5 h
- **Depends on:** DT-03
- **Plan:** [dark-tone.md](../../plan/dark-tone.md)

## Scope

User report: dark mode is too dark, more so with the pane gutter from
[pane-gaps](../../plan/pane-gaps.md). Lift every dark surface by the same
small step so the layers, their spacing and the warm neutral tint stay as
they are. Text, accent and semantic colors and the light theme stay unchanged.

## Likely files

- `frontend/src/style.css`
- `frontend/src/lib/terminal.ts` (dark canvas fallback)
- `frontend/test/dark-tone.test.mjs`
- `frontend/test/terminal.test.mjs`

## Acceptance criteria and tests

- Each dark surface is 6 steps lighter per RGB channel: gutter, primary,
  secondary, tertiary, badge and segment thumb.
- Text keeps at least 4.5:1 contrast on all three surface layers, and the
  count tooltip's role colors on the tertiary surface.
- The terminal's dark fallback canvas matches the new `--bg-primary`.
