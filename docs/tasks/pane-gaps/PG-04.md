## Scope

Hovering a splitter showed the native tooltip "Drag to resize; double-click
or press Enter to equalize". Remove it from the shared `Splitter`, which
removes it from every splitter, nested ones included. Keep the `aria-label`.

## Likely files

- `frontend/src/lib/components/common/Splitter.svelte`
- `frontend/test/splitter.test.mjs`

- No splitter has a `title` attribute. Each keeps its `aria-label`, its
  `separator` role and its resize and equalize behavior.
