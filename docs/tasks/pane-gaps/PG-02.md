## Acceptance criteria and tests

- On Linux, Windows and macOS, in light and dark mode, the gutter meets the
  native title bar cleanly and the pane cards are not clipped at the window
  edges.
- The sidebar, session list and terminal panel splitters drag, equalize on
  double-click and Enter, and resize with the arrow keys. Each shows the
  accent line on hover and focus.
- The maximized terminal panel, the left dock, the collapsed navigation and
  the stale-host overlay look right.
- Nested splitters (file history, problems panel, split terminals) still show
  the hairline.
