# PG-01 — Pane cards on a gutter with gap splitters

- **Status:** ✅ Done
- **Estimate:** 3 h
- **Depends on:** None
- **Plan:** [pane-gaps.md](../../plan/pane-gaps.md)

## Scope

Show the main panes as rounded cards on a darker gutter, with the main
splitters in the gaps and a grip at rest. Keep the hairline for nested
splitters. Account for the gaps in the width guard and in the navigation and
transcript equalization.

## Likely files

- `frontend/src/style.css`
- `frontend/src/App.svelte`
- `frontend/src/lib/components/common/Splitter.svelte`
- `frontend/src/lib/stores/preferences.svelte.ts`
- `frontend/test/splitter.test.mjs`

## Acceptance criteria and tests

- `--bg-gutter` in light and dark themes and `--pane-gap` (6px).
- The workspace is padded by one gap on the gutter. Panes and the terminal
  panel have a border, a radius and clipping. The nav rail is separated by a
  margin instead of a border.
- `Splitter` has a `gap` prop. The three `App.svelte` splitters use it and
  the nested ones do not.
- The left-dock grid's splitter columns are one gap wide.
- `ensureTranscriptVisible` subtracts `LIMITS.paneGaps`. Equalizing the
  navigation and the transcript sums the panes' own widths.
- Unit test for the width guard with gaps, and a source test for the
  splitter wiring.

## Evidence

Done:

- Added `--bg-gutter` (light `#dde2e9`, dark `#1a1a19`) and `--pane-gap: 6px` to `style.css`.
- In `App.svelte`, the workspace now has the gap padding and the gutter background, and `.shell` no longer has a background. `.pane` and `.workspace > .terminal-panel` have a border, `--radius-md` and `overflow: hidden`. The nav rail's `border-right` is now a `margin-right` of one gap. The left-dock grid's splitter columns are `var(--pane-gap)` wide. The sidebar, session list and terminal panel splitters use `gap`. Equalizing the transcript sums the `.pane` widths next to the splitter.
- `Splitter.svelte` has a `gap` prop. In gap mode the splitter takes the gap's space and shows a three-dot grip. On hover, drag or keyboard focus it shows a full-length 2px accent line.
- Added `LIMITS.paneGaps = 24` to `preferences.svelte.ts`. `ensureTranscriptVisible` subtracts it.
