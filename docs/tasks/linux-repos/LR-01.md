## Scope

In `agent-sessions-web`, on a preview deployment:

- Add a Worker entry point beside the static assets, with
  `assets.run_worker_first` for `/api/*`, `/apt/*` and `/rpm/*`. Check that
  every other path, the 404 page and `static/_headers` behave as before.
- Bind Hyperdrive and run one query against the maintainer's Postgres.
  Choose the client (`postgres` or `pg`) and note why.
- Measure the CPU time of the rebuild's heavy parts: gzip of a 500 KB file,
  SHA256 of 2 MB, and an Ed25519 OpenPGP clear-signature with `openpgp` on
  50 KB. Decide whether the free plan is enough or Workers Paid is needed.

## Likely files

`web/wrangler.jsonc`, `web/src/worker.ts`, `web/package.json`.

- The website's Playwright suite passes unchanged against the preview.
