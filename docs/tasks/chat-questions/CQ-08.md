# CQ-08 — Late answers when Codex goes on without one

- **Status:** ✅ Done
- **Estimate:** 3 h
- **Depends on:** CQ-06
- **Plan:** [chat-questions.md](../../plan/chat-questions.md)

## Why

The user reported: with Codex, the question's options appear and the card
closes at once, while the agent's text keeps streaming. The `codex-acp` 2.6.0
code shows two ways this happens (see
[chat-questions-adapters.md](../../plan/chat-questions-adapters.md)):

- a blocking question whose `autoResolutionMs` is short or missing is
  withdrawn after that long, at once when missing;
- an async question (`request_user_input_async`, Codex 0.167's preferred
  tool) never stops the turn, and is withdrawn when the turn completes.

The chat then dropped the card. The store also dropped every question at
`turn_end`, even one the backend still waits on.

## Scope

- Store: a withdrawn question stays in `chat.questions`, marked late, after
  its "went on without an answer" line; `turn_end` no longer drops
  questions (their resolution does). `sendLate` sends the answers as the
  next prompt; `dismissLate` drops the card. Exit and disconnect still clear
  everything.
- `chatQuestions.ts`: `lateBlocker` (problems block, required questions do
  not, one answer is needed) and `lateMessage` (each answered question and its
  answer as text; hidden answers left out).
- `QuestionCard.svelte`: a late card says the agent went on without the
  answer; Submit becomes "Send as message", disabled with the reason while
  the agent works; Skip becomes Dismiss; the draft is kept across the change.
- LiveTail: late cards do not make the chat "wait for your answer".
- Mock: Codex's question can be withdrawn at once while the reply streams,
  as reported.

## Acceptance and tests

- Store tests: withdrawn → line plus late card; `turn_end` keeps a pending
  question; `sendLate` prompts with the text and drops the card; dismiss.
- `chatQuestions` tests for `lateBlocker` and `lateMessage`.
- DOM card test: a question becoming late keeps its draft, Send is disabled
  while working and sends a prompt once idle, Dismiss.
- `npm test`, `npm run check`, `npm run build`; a mock run in the browser.
- Not covered here: a real Codex run (CQ-07, needs approval).

## Evidence

What was done:

- `stores/chats.svelte.ts`: `question_resolved` with `withdrawn` marks the
  card `late` (type `ChatPendingQuestion`) and still adds the "went on
  without an answer" line; any other outcome drops it as before. `turn_end`
  no longer clears questions. `sendLate` prompts with the text and then drops
  the card; `dismissLate` drops it. Exit and disconnect clear every card.
- `chatQuestions.ts`: `lateBlocker`, `lateMessage` ("My answer(s) to your
  question(s):" then "- <question> — <answer>" lines, hidden answers left
  out) and `asksWhileWorking` (Codex `delivery: "async"`).
- `QuestionCard.svelte`: the late card says "<Agent> asked" and "<Agent> went
  on without your answer. You can still send it as a message.", drops the
  countdown and the required marks, keeps the draft (the keyed card is not
  remounted), shows "Send as message", disabled with "<Agent> is still
  working. Send your answers once it finishes." until the chat is idle, and
  Dismiss in place of Skip. An error from a reply that raced the withdrawal
  is cleared when the card goes late.
- `LiveTail.svelte`: late and async questions do not make the status say
  "waits for your answer".
- Mock: "async" in a Codex prompt asks an async question, keeps streaming,
  and withdraws it at the end of the turn; a late answer is a plain prompt.
