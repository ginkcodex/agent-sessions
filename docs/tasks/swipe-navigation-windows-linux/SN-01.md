- **Status:** ✅ Done

## Scope

`hook_window_side_buttons` also adds a `GtkEventControllerScroll` (both
axes, capture phase) to the web view. The `scroll` handler always returns
`FALSE`. Only `GDK_SOURCE_TOUCHPAD` scrolls without Ctrl count. A gesture
starts on `scroll-begin` or after a 200 ms gap, and ends on `scroll-end`.
Deltas are summed in pixels (wheel units × 40). At 80 px sideways, more than
twice the vertical, it calls `goSwipeNavigate` once with the scroll's
direction (negative x: back). Comments and TECH-NOTES no longer say
"macOS only".

  `GtkEventControllerScroll` (both axes) with a per-view `Swipe` state

  non-touchpad devices and Ctrl, starts a gesture after `scroll-begin` or a
  200 ms gap, scales wheel units by 40, and calls `goSwipeNavigate` once at
  80 px sideways, more than twice the vertical (positive x: forward).
- `mousenav.go`, `mousenav_native.go`: comments say macOS and Linux.

  - `mousenav_linux.c` alone compiles with `-Wall -Wextra -Werror`
