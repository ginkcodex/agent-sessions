## Scope

- `SwipeGuard` gets an optional detector, on only on Windows. Per gesture
  it adds up `deltaX`/`deltaY`. A gesture with Shift+wheel, a `deltaMode`
  other than pixels, or a delta of a whole wheel step (≥ 100 px) is no
  swipe. At 80 px sideways, more than twice the vertical, over at least 3
  events, it fires once (negative x: back), unless the gesture scrolled
  something.
- `subscribeMouseNavigate` delivers those swipes like `nav:swipe`, and stops
  on unsubscribe.
- `scrollsSideways` counts `<input>` and `<textarea>` with room left,
  whatever their overflow.

## Acceptance criteria and tests

- Unit tests: a touchpad-like gesture swipes once each way; a gesture that
  scrolled something does not, also at the edge; a new gesture at the edge
  does; vertical, Shift+wheel, line-mode, whole-step and too-short gestures
  do not; pinch ignored; detector off by default; unsubscribe stops it;
  a text field with room counts as scrolling.

## Evidence

- `frontend/src/lib/swipeNavigation.ts`:
  - `SwipeGuard(scrolls, detect)`: with `detect` on, `track` adds up each
    gesture's `deltaX`/`deltaY`. Shift, a `deltaMode` other than 0, or a
    delta of `WHEEL_STEP` (100 px) marks the gesture as a mouse's. After
    `SWIPE_MIN_EVENTS` (3) events, at `SWIPE_DISTANCE` (80 px) sideways and
    more than twice the vertical, it fires once to `onSwipe` listeners
    (negative x: back), unless the gesture scrolled something.
  - `windowSwipeGuard()` turns detect on when `navigator.platform` starts
    with `Win` and passes `deltaY`, `deltaMode` and `shiftKey`.
  - `scrollsSideways` also counts `INPUT` and `TEXTAREA` with room left,
    whatever their overflow.
- `frontend/src/lib/api.ts`: `subscribeMouseNavigate` also subscribes to
  `guard.onSwipe` and unsubscribes it.
- `TECH-NOTES.md`: the Windows two-finger swipe note.

  Shift, line mode, a whole horizontal or vertical step; pinch; a gesture
