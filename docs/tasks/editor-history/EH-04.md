# EH-04 — Check in the desktop app

- **Status:** 🟡 Partial (macOS side buttons only; Windows, Linux and the other release criteria not reported)
- **Estimate:** 1 h
- **Depends on:** EH-03
- **Plan:** [editor-history.md](../../plan/editor-history.md)

## Scope

In the desktop app, on a real mouse with side buttons (Windows WebView2 and
Linux WebKitGTK at least): walk the plan's release criteria. Pick files,
switch tabs, follow a Markdown link in the preview, go to a definition; step
back and forward with the side buttons, the keys and the toolbar. Close and
reopen the dialog, switch projects and back, and check each keeps its own
history.

## Acceptance criteria and tests

- Every release criterion in the plan observed in the app, per platform
  tried; platforms not tried are listed as not checked.

## Evidence

- 2026-11-07, macOS, reported by the user: the mouse's side buttons step
  back and forward in Go to File, as they do in the session content.
- Not checked: Windows (WebView2) and Linux (WebKitGTK); the keys and the
  toolbar, the Markdown preview round trip and per-project history were
  not reported separately.
- A three-finger swipe did nothing on that Mac: macOS uses three fingers
  for its own gestures there, so no swipe reaches the app. Two-finger
  swipes are planned in [swipe-navigation](../swipe-navigation/README.md).
